Hymn

A Call Comes Ringing

Words: Harriet E. Jones; rev. Ryan Daniel Cornett

Music: William Walker; add. Ryan Daniel Cornett

Tune: INVITATUS

Meter: C.M. w/ Refrain

Listen & sing

SATB · E major · 4/4 · Suggested tempo: 144 BPM

Sheet music PDF Freely given for private, family, and congregational worship. No strings attached.

FacebookEmail

Stanza 1

A call comes ringing from the throne;
O Christian, do you hear?
Sounding for all the Father's own:
"Go, preach a Kingdom near."

Refrain

Go forth in holy armor clad,
Christ's crimson flag unfurled;
Heeding the call, your Sov'reign's call,
Go forth and win the world.

Stanza 2

It was for this His life He gave,
For sinners crucified;
Gathering near lost souls to save,
Go tell it far and wide.

Stanza 3

Go forth, a brave, united band,
All girded for the fray;
Stand firm until you take the land,
And thus the call obey.

About the song

I found this obscure hymn text from Jones, a call to united Christian witness under Christ's authority, and felt it deserved new life. I decided to pair it with a little used Walker tune that I fell in love with the first time I heard it.

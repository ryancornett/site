Stanza 1

Through tests and trials, I believe,
Lord, help my unbelief:
That in the darkness I'll perceive
Thy nearness in my grief.

Stanza 2

When field is bare and face is gaunt,
Twice-greeted are mine eyes
With vanity of all I want
And good Thy hand supplies.

Stanza 3

Though prayers that seem unanswered roll,
And night conceals Thine arm,
Thy unseen care preserves my soul,
Keeps safe from hidden harm.

Stanza 4

How bright the beams of joy to know
That on the darkest day,
Thy Son resolved through death to go
And light the Heav'nly way.

Stanza 5

Thus when the trial's shadow falls,
I shall not languish low;
'Tis then from stores in Heaven's halls
Thy blessings overflow.

About the song

Through Tests and Trials expresses the believer's struggle to trust God amid suffering, uncertainty, and seemingly unanswered prayer. Through seasons of grief and deprivation, the hymn affirms God's unseen providence, the sustaining hope found in Christ's suffering, and the abundant spiritual blessings that flow from His grace.

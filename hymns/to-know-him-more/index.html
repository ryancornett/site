Stanza 1

The blessed morn I greet with prayer,
A seed in Heaven sown;
My daily cross I rise to bear,
And bear it not alone.

Refrain

To know Him more, to know Him more,
My respite and my light;
To seek to know the Savior more
Yields treasures of delight.

Stanza 2

The Word that pierces joint and bone,
With holy fire renews:
Hid'n in my heart, its truth I own;
A pearl I cannot lose.

Stanza 3

For pardon, aid, and purer thought
I ask on bended knee;
The Lord, my praying changes not,
But oh, it changes me.

Stanza 4

When gathered 'round to feast and sing,
To bless and praise with awe,
In seeking closeness to our King,
We closer all will draw.

About the song

Emphasizing the transforming power of the ordinary means of grace, this hymn reminds us that the greatest treasure of Christian devotion is knowing the Savior more intimately, and we can do so in the simple, daily spiritual disciplines.

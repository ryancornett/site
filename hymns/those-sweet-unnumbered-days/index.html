Those Sweet, Unnumbered Days

Words: Ryan Daniel Cornett

Meter: L.M.

Stanza 1

Those sweet, unnumbered days to come,
Whose bounds creation cannot sum,
May hold our years on earth made new,
But for God's love, they are too few.

Stanza 2

The ocean's vast, unmeasured deep
Could not within its boundaries keep
The ever-surging, gracious flood
That swells to raise the saints of God.

Stanza 3

Above the ancient mountain's height
And fading hues of evening light,
God's lasting mercies higher stand,
Each morning offered by His hand.

Stanza 4

Though battered by the harshest gale,
The storms of death shall not prevail;
God's winds of favor make my course
And take me home to Heaven's shores.

About the song

Those Sweet, Unnumbered Days celebrates the immeasurable love, grace, and mercy of God, whose blessings exceed the vastness of creation and endure beyond the limits of time. Through images of boundless oceans, towering mountains, and tempestuous seas, the hymn expresses the believer's confidence in God's unfailing faithfulness, sustaining grace, and promise of eternal life in His presence.

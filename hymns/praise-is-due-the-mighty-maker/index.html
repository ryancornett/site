Hymn

Praise Is Due the Mighty Maker

Words: Ryan Daniel Cornett

Music: W. Irving Hartshorn

Stanza 1

Praise the Maker of all wonders,
He whose word called forth the light,
Echoing like a thousand thunders
In the stars that spangle night.
See the heavens highly vaulted
Bow in awe, then take their stand
To proclaim our God exalted
Who upholds them by His hand.

Refrain

Praise is due the Mighty Maker,
Lord of oceans, land, and skies;
Sov'reign King of man and nature,
Let our song to Him arise.

Stanza 2

Praise the Maker of all beauty,
Who is pleased to fill our sight
Not with goods fit but for duty,
Nay, with visions of delight:
Clouds that crown the snowy mountain,
Soaring tree and fading flow'r,
Dancing flame and sparkling fountain
Show the kindness in His pow'r.

Stanza 3

Praise the Maker everlasting,
Whose dominion marches on;
May the earth and heavens vast sing
Of the ancient, ageless One.
Peaks will fall and suns will tire,
Storms will quiet across the plains;
Sand and stone will melt in fire,
Yet our peerless God remains.

About the song

This modernized hymn celebrates God's sovereign power in creation, His generous gift of beauty throughout the natural world, and His everlasting dominion over all things. From the first light of creation to the passing away of heaven and earth, we are called to praise the One who made and sustains it all.

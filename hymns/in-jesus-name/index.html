About the song

In Jesus' Name is a congregational call to prayer, leading believers through adoration, confession, thanksgiving, and supplication (A.C.T.S.) as they approach the Heavenly Father through Jesus Christ. With its repeated declaration of Christ's exalted name, the song emphasizes His unique authority as our Mediator and the privilege of bringing every prayer before God through Him.

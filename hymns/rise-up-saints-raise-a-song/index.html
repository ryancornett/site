Rise Up Saints, Raise a Song

Words: Ryan Daniel Cornett

Stanza 1

Rise up, saints, raise a song to your King:
He whose scepter holds sway ever vast,
To whom centuries are brief as a day,
The First and the Last.

Refrain

For the Father and the Son,
Holy Spirit, Three in One,
We are gathered, off'ring praise
To the Ancient of Days.

Stanza 2

Lay aside earthly treasures and woes;
Come partake of the grace freely shared
That the Giver of every good gift
Has, for us, prepared.

Stanza 3

Now to His Kingdom not of this world
He will keep His beloved Bride true,
Till with one voice we saints sing her song
When all is made new.

About the song

Rise Up, Saints is a joyful call to worship, celebrating the eternal reign of our triune God. Inviting believers to set aside earthly cares and receive His abundant grace, the hymn looks forward to the day when Christ's redeemed Church will unite in everlasting praise as He makes all things new.

About Ryan

  • Elder at Manchester Baptist Church, a confessional, Southern Baptist church in eastern Kentucky, since October 2019 serving to teach, preach, plan and execute evangelistic efforts, structure the worship services, and lead the songs.
  • Hymn and songwriter with a focus on theologically-rich lyrics and tunes that encourage participation in corporate worship.
  • Weekend homesteader, web developer, and ham radio enthusiast. "I contacted a station in Italy with a wire antenna hung in a walnut tree in my backyard!"
  • Husband to BreAnne and father to two daughters.
  • "My prayer is that some of the resources available here—whether a hymn about justification, or one about repentance; a Bible plan generator; or online audio Bible—will bless you and be useful in your walk with the Lord. Soli Deo Gloria!"

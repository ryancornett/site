02 / A little about me

A servant of Christ.
A song to share.

I'm Ryan—a husband, father, church elder, hymn writer, and hobby developer in eastern Kentucky.

I've served as an elder at Manchester Baptist Church, a confessional Southern Baptist church, since October 2019. My work includes teaching, preaching, evangelism, planning worship services, and leading songs.

My hymns focus on theologically rich lyrics and tunes that encourage participation in corporate worship. Away from that work, I’m a weekend homesteader, web developer, and ham radio enthusiast. I once contacted a station in Italy with a wire antenna hung in a walnut tree in my backyard!

I'm husband to BreAnne and father to two daughters, Lydia & Abigail.

01

19point9↗ (opens in a new tab)

A game to test your timing skills

About this project

Tap (mouse click or space bar) as close to the target times as possible. Can you tap close enough to the targets for a delta less than one second? If so, you've got skills!

03

BSB Audio on Youtube↗ (opens in a new tab)

Listen to the Berean Standard Bible by chapter and book with YouTube podcasts

About this project

Listen to the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) by chapter and book with YouTube podcasts. Your spot is saved on your desired mobile device so you can pick back up right where you left off. You can also download for offline listening.

04

Custom Bible Plan↗ (opens in a new tab)

Create your very own Bible reading plan

About this project

Choose your plan name, start date, end date, what days of the week you want to read, the time of day you want to be alerted, and the books of the Bible you want to read in your desired order. Pick from printed, CSV, and iCal formats (I use iCal and import to my Google calendar).

05

Dot Dot Node↗ (opens in a new tab)

A daily strategy puzzle game

About this project

Goal: fill every node with dots. But be careful! You can fill in only one direction at a time. You win if every node has a dot, but try to to do it with the lowest time and without assists.

06

Gridle↗ (opens in a new tab)

A daily logic puzzle grid game

About this project

Guess the secret 5x5 layout in 6 attempts. Edge numbers show how many blocks are in that line except for one hidden row and one hidden column (marked with a "?"). Toggle squares purple, then Submit your guess.

07

KR4BJN↗ (opens in a new tab)

My amateur radio station site

About this project

I made a fun little page that I hope inspires others to pick up the hobby! Here you can see what I like to do on the air, how I get on the air, the ARRL transmission schedule, Parks on the Air (POTA) activations in my region, and current band conditions. I also built a practice oscillator so you can try out Morse code!

08

Lofi Bible↗ (opens in a new tab)

About this project

Read and listen to the BSB or KJV audio Bible with background lofi chill beats and/or lofi "hymns." This was one of the first big hobby projects I finished and the first to garner a donation. (Thanks, Logan!)
